**Every death leaves something behind.**

The first release under the name Afterself. Your dead come back at night with your face and your voice, to stalk you, rob you, trap your friends and take your place.

> [!WARNING]
> Horror mod with jumpscares, loud screams and flashing red images. `screamerIntense=false` gives a calm screamer, and `screamer=false` turns it off on your client.

## What's in it

- Souls that take your items when you die, exist only at night, and come back for you every night.
- Hunting by sound: sprinting, talking and shouting in Voice Chat give you away. When a soul loses you, it searches the last place it saw you.
- Scares: footsteps behind you, doors and chests moving on their own, torches knocked down, your own voice from hours ago, and a soul at the edge of your bed.
- Looting from chests and from players, with the loot equipped at once.
- Player-like combat: critical hits, shields, bows, ender pearls, bridging, pillaring, digging, running away to eat.
- Traps where a soul looks exactly like the dead player to draw others in.
- Possession: a soul that kills you during a trap walks your body to the other players and turns on them.
- Six screamers with your own face, rare on purpose.
- Voice clips only with each player's consent, erasable at any time.

## Changes since SILENCE 0.3.4

- **New name.** The mod id is now `afterself` and the command is `/afterself`. Souls, voice clips and settings from a world played with SILENCE are not carried over.
- **Logo** in the Forge and NeoForge mod list.
- **License:** GPL-3.0-only. The mod used to be All Rights Reserved.
- **The mod list** now shows the right version and an up-to-date description.

## Tested

The in-game test suite (about 180 checks) passes on Forge 1.19.2, Forge and NeoForge 1.20.1, Forge and NeoForge 1.21, and Forge and NeoForge 1.21.1. Dedicated servers start on 1.20.1, 1.21 and 1.21.1. A 1.19.2 dedicated server has not been tested.
